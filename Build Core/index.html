About Build Core

Building homes, not just houses.

Founded in Karachi in 2012, Build Core has grown into one of the city's most trusted names in residential construction. We don't build high-rises. We don't do commercial. What we do — and do exceptionally well — is build homes that families cherish for generations.

From a 120 sq. yard house in Gulshan to a 2000 sq. yard villa in DHA Phase 8, every project receives the same obsessive attention to detail, the same quality materials, and the same promise: your home, delivered on time.

How We Work

From land to living room.

A transparent, four-stage process that keeps you informed at every step.

01

Consultation

We visit your plot, understand your vision, budget, and timeline. No obligations — just a real conversation about your dream home.

02

Design

Our architects draft 2D/3D plans tailored to your plot. You see your home before a single brick is laid — and revise until it's perfect.

03

Construction

Our in-house team breaks ground. Weekly progress reports, site visits, and a dedicated project manager keep you in the loop.

04

Handover

Final walkthrough, snag list, keys in hand. Plus a 5-year structural warranty because we stand behind every home we build.
